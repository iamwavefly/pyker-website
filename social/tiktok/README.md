# Pyker on TikTok: profile kit

Everything needed to set up the Pyker TikTok profile.

## Profile photo

- **Use:** `pyker-tiktok-avatar.png` (1080×1080, brand purple `#6B4EFF`, white P mark)
- **Alternative:** `pyker-tiktok-avatar-dark.png` (same mark on `#0C0A1A`), only if purple clashes with a campaign
- The mark sits inside TikTok's circle crop with room to spare and stays readable down to 48px.

## Account type

Switch to a **Business account**: Profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy → Account → Switch to Business Account.
That unlocks the website link in the bio, analytics, and auto-replies to DMs.

- **Category:** the closest finance option in the list (e.g. Financial services / Finance)

## Name and username

| Field | Value | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | `Pyker: Crypto to Naira` | 22 of 30 chars. The name is searchable, so the keyword helps people find us. |
| Username | `@usepyker` | Matches usepyker.com. Fallbacks: `@pyker.ng`, `@pykerapp`, `@pyker_app` |

## Bio (80 characters max)

All three lead with Pyker's promise, **Never settle for less**: more naira for your crypto, better FX on the dollar card, low fees.
**Pick one.** Line breaks are allowed; keep the 👇 last so it points at the link.

1. **Recommended** (76):
   ```
   Never settle for less 💸
   More naira for your crypto. Better FX on your card 👇
   ```
2. (73)
   ```
   Never settle for less. Low spread, low fees, more naira for your crypto 👇
   ```
3. Pidgin (65):
   ```
   No dey sell your crypto cheap 💸 More naira, better FX, low fees 👇
   ```

Avoid "best rate in Nigeria" or "cheapest" unless you can show the comparison: claims that you beat competitors need proof.

## Website link

```
https://usepyker.com/?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bio
```

The UTM tags make TikTok visitors show up as their own source in analytics.

## Other profile links

- **Instagram / YouTube:** connect them once those accounts are live (Edit profile → Social).
- **Contact:** set the business email to `support@usepyker.com`.

## Auto-reply for DMs

Settings and privacy → Business suite → Direct messages → Welcome message:

```
Hi 👋 Thanks for reaching out to Pyker!
For account help, chat with us on WhatsApp: 0703 077 2726 or email support@usepyker.com.
We will NEVER ask for your PIN, password, OTP or seed phrase.
```

The last line matters: fake "support" accounts target crypto users in the comments and DMs.

## Before posting

- TikTok doesn't allow paid ads or paid creator promos for crypto trading, but normal posts about the app are fine. Keep posts educational and factual. No "guaranteed returns" or "get rich" claims.
- Pin 3 videos to the top of the profile once posted:
  1. **Never settle for less:** quote the same amount of USDT on Pyker and another app at the same moment, then show the naira that lands. Use real, unedited quotes.
  2. **The dollar card FX:** fund the card and show the rate and fee on screen before paying for Netflix or ChatGPT.
  3. **What Pyker does in 20 seconds:** sell USDT, and the credit alert lands (screen recording).
